Monarchs of Roman Empire (4)

27 BCE – 14 CE

Imperator Caesar Augustus

First Emperor of the Roman Empire, initiating two centuries of Pax Romana, reforming taxation, establishing the Praetorian Guard, and rebuilding Rome in marble.

Read Bio →
98–117 CE

Emperor Trajan

One of the 'Five Good Emperors', leading successful military conquests of Dacia and Parthia to expand the Roman Empire to its maximum territorial extent in history.

Read Bio →
161–180 CE

Emperor Marcus Aurelius

Last of the Five Good Emperors, celebrated Stoic philosopher who authored 'Meditations' while defending the empire's northern frontier along the Danube.

Read Bio →
306–337 CE

Constantine I (The Great)

Roman Emperor who legalized Christianity with the Edict of Milan (313 CE) and moved the imperial capital to Constantinople, founding the Byzantine millennium.
